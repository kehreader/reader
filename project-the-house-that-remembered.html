The House That Remembered

AuthorClara Bennett
GenreSupernatural Horror
Length74,000 words

Manuscript Excerpt

From Chapter One — "The House"

Daniel remembered the house by its sounds — the grandfather clock, the kitchen pipes, the third stair, his grandmother's footsteps. He hadn't been back in fifteen years, and standing outside the front door, he wondered why he'd ever agreed to return. Inside, a photograph hung above the fireplace: his grandmother, young, standing beside a man he'd never seen before. When he reached for it, it fell and the glass cracked. Four words were written across the back: He never left this house.

The grandfather clock stopped at 12:00 — fifteen minutes fast by his phone. He laughed it off. Then he heard footsteps upstairs, one, two, three, heading toward his old bedroom. He climbed the stairs anyway. His grandmother's room was empty, and smelled of lavender, just as he remembered. Then the door behind him closed on its own, and the handle wouldn't move.

A whisper came from inside the wardrobe: "Daniel." The wardrobe door opened by itself. Inside was a photograph — of Daniel, standing in that exact room, taken that same morning.

Overall Impression

The manuscript has strong atmospheric horror. The house feels like more than a location, which is essential for this type of story.

Strong Points

  • Excellent setting.
  • Effective use of sound.
  • Strong family history.
  • Good supernatural mystery.
  • Chapter ending creates immediate curiosity.

Suggested Improvements

The grandmother should have a larger presence through memories, and Daniel's childhood experiences in the house could become important later. Consider giving the house specific rules — certain rooms that can't be entered after midnight, certain objects that should never be moved, a house that reacts to people who lie. This would make the supernatural elements feel consistent rather than random.

9/10
